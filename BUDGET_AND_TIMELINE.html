PART 1: BUDGET ALLOCATION

1.1 Executive Summary

This document outlines the budget allocation and implementation timeline for the XGIMI MoGo 4 digital marketing strategy. The approach prioritises cost-effectiveness and free/low-cost tools to validate the venture while maintaining professional execution. The project period runs from 10th January 2026 to 18th March 2026. The total project budget is designed to be 0–190,000 UGX (Uganda Shillings) over the 10-week period, with all tools either free or minimally priced. Exchange reference: ~3,800 UGX per 1 USD.

1.2 Budget Philosophy & Principles

PrincipleDescription
Lean-FirstMaximise free tier tools before allocating paid budget
Validation-DrivenSpend only after validating channel performance
ScalableBudget structure allows incremental increases as ROI proves
TransparentAll costs documented with clear allocation rationale
