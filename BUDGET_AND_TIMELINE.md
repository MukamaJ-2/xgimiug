# PART 1: BUDGET ALLOCATION

## 1.1 Executive Summary

This document outlines the budget allocation and implementation timeline for the XGIMI MoGo 4 digital marketing strategy. The approach prioritises **cost-effectiveness** and **free/low-cost tools** to validate the venture while maintaining professional execution. The total monthly budget is designed to be **$0–25 USD**, with flexibility to scale based on validation results.

---

## 1.2 Budget Philosophy & Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Lean-First** | Maximise free tier tools before allocating paid budget |
| **Validation-Driven** | Spend only after validating channel performance |
| **Scalable** | Budget structure allows incremental increases as ROI proves |
| **Transparent** | All costs documented with clear allocation rationale |
