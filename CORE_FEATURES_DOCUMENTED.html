Executive Summary

The XGIMI MoGo 4 is a compact, battery-powered portable projector featuring Google TV, 1080p Full HD resolution, and up to 5 hours of movie playback. This document provides detailed documentation of the seven core solution features as presented on the official landing page and product specifications.

Feature 1: 550 ISO Lumens Brightness

Overview

The MoGo 4 delivers 550 ISO Lumens of brightness, enabling clear, vivid Full HD projection on screen sizes up to 120 inches. This brightness level ensures that viewers can enjoy high-quality visuals without requiring complete darkness.

Technical Specifications

Use Cases

  • Indoor Movie Nights: Living room or bedroom projection without blackout curtains
  • Outdoor Camping: Backyard or camping trips in dusk/twilight conditions
  • Gaming: Responsive, bright gameplay with minimal lag
  • Presentations: Small-group business or educational presentations

User Benefits

  • No need for total darkness
  • Clear visibility of text and fine details
  • Reduced eye strain compared to dimmer projectors
  • Consistent color and clarity across the projected image

Feature 3: 110% BT.2020 & 1000:1 Contrast

Overview

The MoGo 4 supports a wide color gamut (110% BT.2020) and 1000:1 native contrast ratio, delivering cinematic color accuracy and deep blacks for a premium viewing experience.

Technical Specifications

SpecificationValue
Color Gamut110% BT.2020
Contrast Ratio1000:1 (Native)
Color StandardDCI-P3 90%
Display TechnologyDLP

What BT.2020 Means

BT.2020 (Rec. 2020) is an international standard for ultra-high-definition television. Achieving 110% coverage means the projector reproduces colors beyond the standard gamut, resulting in more vibrant reds, greens, and blues.

User Benefits

  • Professional-grade color accuracy
  • Enhanced viewing immersion
  • Support for HDR-compatible content
  • Reduced color banding and artifacts
